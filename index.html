HEURISTIC MONITORING LOGIC

Explainable Clinical Rules

Instead of opaque black-box models, anomaly detection operates on transparent, verifiable clinical threshold heuristics derived from NASA-STD-3001 Bioastronautics Standards:

  • Rule CDSS-O2-01: Acute Arterial Hypoxemia (SpO₂ < 92.0% for >= 2 consecutive 1s readings).
  • Rule CDSS-HR-01: Sustained Exertional/Compensatory Tachycardia (HR > 115 bpm).
  • Rule CDSS-ENV-01: Cabin Hypercapnia Limit (pCO₂ > 3.0 mmHg @ 760 mmHg).
  • Concordance Check: Distinguishes sensor decoupling from real pathology by cross-correlating SpO₂ with respiration and heart rate.
AURA AI ARCHITECTURE

6-Part Structured CDSS

AURA serves as an onboard Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) for non-physician crew. To guarantee clinical rigor, every evaluation outputs six structured dimensions:

  1. Observation: Objective telemetry readings and reported symptoms.
  2. Possible Significance: Underlying spaceflight physiological mechanism.
  3. Data-Quality Checks: Motion artifact and sensor decoupling verification.
  4. Suggested Next Action: Protocolized, non-prescriptive countermeasures.
  5. Escalation Guidance: Criteria for alerting the Crew Medical Officer (CMO).
  6. Scientific Reference: Authoritative NASA/ESA standards citations.
VALIDATION & ROADMAP

Limitations & Future Plans

Separating current demonstration capabilities from operational spacecraft flight certification:

  • Current Status (TRL 4): Component and functional validation in simulated browser environment.

  • Roadmap to TRL 7: Integration with physical Astroskin / Bio-Harness BLE hardware, edge micro-controller encryption, and formal clinical flight qualification.

Structured Clinical Rules Specification

CDSS-RULE-O2-01 CRITICAL

Acute Arterial Hypoxemia

CONDITION: spo2 < 92.0% for >= 2 consecutive 1-second readings
Quality Check: Evaluates PPG perfusion index (PI > 0.5%) and correlates with respiratory rate elevation to exclude probe disconnect.
Action: Check suit umbilical seals, switch to emergency 100% O2 purge loop, remain calm to reduce oxygen consumption.
Escalation: Notify Flight Surgeon and EVA buddy immediately; abort spacewalk if SpO2 fails to recover within 60 seconds.
Standard: NASA-STD-3001 Vol 2 §6.3; FAA Office of Commercial Space Transportation Physiological Guidelines.
CDSS-RULE-HR-01 WARNING

Sustained Resting Tachycardia

CONDITION: heart_rate > 115.0 bpm at rest (workload == 0)
Quality Check: Inspects Lead-II R-R interval consistency to differentiate true sinus tachycardia from muscle EMG motion artifact.
Action: Sit upright or strap into sleep station, hydrate with 500 mL electrolyte solution, practice paced slow breathing.
Escalation: Alert Crew Medical Officer if resting heart rate remains > 115 bpm after 10 minutes of complete physical rest.
Standard: NASA-STD-3001 Vol 1 §4.1; Hughson et al. (2018).
CDSS-RULE-ENV-01 WARNING

Cabin Atmospheric Hypercapnia

CONDITION: (cabin_co2 / 100) * cabin_pressure > 3.00 mmHg (0.39% vol @ 760 mmHg)
Quality Check: Cross-verifies primary and redundant non-dispersive infrared (NDIR) carbon dioxide sensors.
Action: Increase cabin ventilation fans in sleeping quarters, verify Carbon Dioxide Removal Assembly (CDRA) scrubber bed cycle.
Escalation: Notify Mission Control Flight Directors to reconfigure environmental life-support scrubbers.
Standard: NASA-STD-3001 Vol 2 §6.2.2; Law et al. (2014).
CDSS-RULE-RAD-01 CRITICAL

High-Flux Ionizing Radiation Event

CONDITION: radiation > 5.00 mSv/day
Quality Check: Correlates silicon detector rates with external SPE magnetometer alert channels.
Action: Relocate crew to central storm shelter (polyethylene shielded core / water-wall compartments).
Escalation: Coordinate with Space Radiation Analysis Group (SRAG) at Johnson Space Center for solar flare trajectory updates.
Standard: NASA-STD-3001 Vol 1 §4.4; Cucinotta et al. (2013).
CDSS-RULE-SANS-01 WARNING

SANS Cephalad Fluid Redistribution & Optic Sheath Expansion

CONDITION: ONSD > 5.80 mm OR acute dilation > 0.5 mm above pre-flight baseline
Quality Check: Requires verification that ultrasound probe is perpendicular to optic nerve axis 3 mm behind retina.
Action: Initiate 30–60 minute session of Lower Body Negative Pressure (LBNP) at -25 mmHg; maintain dietary sodium restriction < 2,000 mg/day.
Escalation: Transmit raw OCT and ultrasound images to ground Flight Surgeon telemedicine queue for expert review.
Standard: NASA Human Research Program SANS Evidence Report; Lee et al. (2020).
CDSS-RULE-CARD-02 CRITICAL

Paroxysmal Ventricular Tachyarrhythmia

CONDITION: heart_rate > 150 bpm AND sudden onset (delta HR > 50 bpm within 3s) AND workload == 0
Quality Check: Analyzes Lead-II baseline noise; confirms absence of preceding exertional work telemetry.
Action: Direct astronaut to assume safe reclined posture, attach continuous 12-lead monitoring, ready Automated External Defibrillator (AED).
Escalation: Immediate urgent alert to Crew Medical Officer and emergency telemetry priority packet to ground flight surgeons.
Standard: PhysioNet MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database (#208); NASA-STD-3001 Vol 1 §4.1.
